## Lessons Learned from AI Collaboration

### Effective AI Collaboration Patterns
1. **Use AI for Structure, Human for Content:** AI excellent for templates and organization, human essential for accuracy
2. **Accelerate Documentation, Not Decision-Making:** AI speeds up writing, human makes all technical and business decisions
3. **Validate Everything Technical:** Never trust AI for system behavior descriptions or technical capabilities
4. **Maintain Evidence Chain Integrity:** AI cannot generate or validate actual measurement data and test results

### AI Limitations Observed
- **No System Understanding:** AI cannot actually test or validate system functionality
- **No Domain Expertise:** AI lacks context for business requirements and technical trade-offs
- **No Quality Judgment:** AI cannot determine evidence sufficiency or professional standards
- **No Accountability:** AI cannot take responsibility for technical accuracy or engineering decisions

### Human Value Demonstrated
- **Technical Judgment:** Complex architecture and design decisions require human expertise
- **Quality Standards:** Professional engineering standards and evidence validation remain human responsibility
- **Honest Communication:** Limitation acknowledgment and scope boundaries require professional integrity
- **System Ownership:** Accountability for system behavior and capability claims must remain with human engineer
